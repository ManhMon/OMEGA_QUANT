IMPLEMENTED: RISK/SIZING OVERLAY

GATE: BASELINE_RISK_SIZING_OPTIMIZATION_20260616

Two independent, opt-in, default-off features:

A) INVERSE-VOLATILITY SIZING
   - Replaces equal-weight within top-N with 1/realized_vol weights
   - PIT-safe: trailing window only (default 20 days)
   - Floor prevents degenerate zero-vol weights (default 0.3)
   - Expected: reduces portfolio vol, improves Sharpe

B) CONFIDENCE SCALING
   - Scales total gross exposure by rolling signal IC strength
   - PIT-safe: trailing window only (default 60 days)
   - Range: [0.3, 1.2] (never fully de-risks, caps upside leverage)
   - Expected: reduces exposure when signal quality degrades

PIT SAFETY: Both features use ONLY trailing data. No current-bar data,
no forward returns, no OOS labels.
