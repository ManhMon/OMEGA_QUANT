Verdict: **LSTM_HURTS**. LSTM head produces NEGATIVE median test IC (-0.0199) across 45 folds. Blend=0.50 pulls fold_SR_ann from 3.671 to 1.204; fold_DSR collapses from 0.374 to 0.089 (well below 0.95 publishable threshold). Ridge head IC unchanged (0.0173) -> Ridge alone is fine, the LSTM head itself is anti-signal at this configuration.

Diagnosis hypotheses (need follow-up to confirm):
- 8 epochs likely under-fit; sequence model needs more training to beat zero.
- Lookback=60 may be too long given 45 fold-blocks (each fold ~244 train bars per ticker after embargo).
- CPCV refit per fold means LSTM trains 45x on small windows -> high variance.
- Blend=0.50 too aggressive for an unproven head.

Decision: keep no-LSTM Phase 5 (fold_SR_ann=3.671, fold_DSR=0.374) as Phase 5 baseline. LSTM extension is **NOT PROMOTED**. Saved comparison: `.ly_state/session/phase5_lstm_comparison.json`.
